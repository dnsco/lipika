The three threads are one group. The third split from the second (its `from:`), which makes them
one project, and the first shares their system. The skill dispatches one curator for the group, not
one per thread. A curator that sees only its own thread cannot judge
whether a sibling absorbed it, and the goal of curation is merging threads. Replaces
`one-curator-per-thread`, which encoded a drift from the ruling "one curator per partition".
